Our Heritage & Craft

The Story Behind 7th Heaven Wakad

Redefining celebration cakes in Wakad through our pioneering 100% Pure Vegetarian Live Kitchen concept.

Crafted with Devotion

Freshness You Can Watch. Taste You Never Forget.

Located at Shop No. 109, Austin Plaza, Mhatoba Chowk, Kaspate Wasti, 7th Heaven Wakad was born out of a simple question: Why should customers settle for pre-made cakes sitting in commercial display chillers for days?

We brought the live kitchen revolution to Wakad. Step into our boutique store and watch our certified pastry chefs hand-whip rich cream, slice moist sponge, layer farm-fresh fruit compotes or molten Belgian chocolate ganache, and pipe your personalized message onto the cake in just 7 minutes.
